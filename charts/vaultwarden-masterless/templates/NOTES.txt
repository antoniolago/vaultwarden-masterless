IMPORTANT — mobile clients need one setting after their first login:
  A passwordless (Key Connector) account has no master password, and the Bitwarden apps
  deliberately log themselves out when an account has neither a master password nor a
  PIN/biometrics ("There is no valid way to unlock this app"). After the first SSO login, users
  must open Settings -> Account security -> Unlock options and enable "Unlock with PIN code" or
  "Unlock with biometrics"; otherwise every app restart costs them a fresh SSO login.
  Details in COMPATIBILITY.md.
